---
title: Arrows in word space
description: >-
  A map of 5,000 English words, placed from real word vectors (spaCy) so that
  words with similar meanings sit close together. Instructions to a coding agent
  type out one at a time, and each word draws an arrow that moves a single point,
  the context, across the map. The context then settles among its nearest words,
  and the agent's steps (such as grep, edit, build, commit, push and merge) carry
  it further. In "Try it" you type your own instruction and run the agent on it.
tags: [ai]
---
